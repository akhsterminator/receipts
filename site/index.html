From feedback to a spec you can defend

Point it at transcripts, support tickets, app reviews or a GitHub repo. Every step is inspectable, and nothing leaves your machine unless you choose a model.

Ingest & redact

CSV, JSON, .vtt, .srt, .docx, GitHub issues. Personal data is removed locally; customers become salted pseudonyms.

Extract & verify

Claude, Ollama or offline heuristics propose quotes. Anything not found word for word in the source is dropped.

Rank

Quotes cluster into opportunities, scored by reach, intensity, segment and recency, with a formula you edit in config.

Write & check

A Markdown spec where each requirement cites quote IDs. receipts check runs locally, in CI and over MCP.

Verified, not generated

Models only propose quotes. Receipts stores the exact substring with offsets and a hash, and re-checks it on every run.

Bets are first-class

Build something without evidence on purpose with bet:. It passes the check, and everyone can see it's a bet.
