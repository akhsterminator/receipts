> **Evidence base:** verified quotes from support tickets, app reviews, an NPS survey and five interviews (all fictional).
> Run `receipts why R1` to read the evidence behind any requirement.

## R1. Let members pause their membership for up to 3 months instead of cancelling

because: [Q-0040, Q-0096, Q-0006, Q-0117, Q-0063, Q-0065, Q-0051, Q-0079]  # 11 customers · 3 segments
metric: Voluntary cancellations on monthly plans −15% within 90 days of launch
owner: Retention squad

> "I would honestly rather pause than cancel but I couldn't find a pause button anywhere in the app." — Q-0040

## R2. Give every member of a Family plan their own profile and history

because: [Q-0030, Q-0106, Q-0120, Q-0125, Q-0016]
metric: Households paying for 2+ separate accounts −30% within two quarters

## R3. Team admins can see seat activation, reassign seats and download one monthly invoice

because: [Q-0022, Q-0029, Q-0088, Q-0112]
metric: Team plan renewal rate ≥ 90%

## R4. Members can download classes to play offline

because: [Q-0036, Q-0027, Q-0091, Q-0100, Q-0094]
metric: Sessions abandoned due to buffering −50%; weekly active travellers +10%

> "Downloading a few classes the night before would be perfect." — Q-0091

## R5. Show live heart rate from Apple Watch during classes

because: [Q-0042]
metric: 25% of iOS members with a watch connect it within 60 days

## R6. Remind members 7 days before a trial ends or an annual plan renews

because: [Q-0045, Q-0070, Q-0110, Q-0064]
metric: Refund requests citing "unexpected charge" −40%

## R7. Generate a personalised weekly workout plan with AI

metric: Weekly active members +10%

## R8. Public API for gym partners

bet: Two gym chains asked for it in sales calls (not in this evidence store). We'll run 5 partner interviews before building more than a prototype.
metric: 2 signed partners by end of Q2
